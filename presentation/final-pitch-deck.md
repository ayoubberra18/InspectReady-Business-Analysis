# Final Pitch Deck — Slide Outline

## Slide 1 — InspectReady
**Know before the inspector arrives.**

AI-powered pre-inspection readiness concept for general contractors.

## Slide 2 — The Problem
Contractors can discover inspection problems only after the municipal inspector arrives, leading to rework, idle crews, repeat visits, and project delays.

## Slide 3 — The Solution
Insert a structured pre-inspection step before the official inspection: capture → analyze → identify issues → fix → verify → readiness report.

## Slide 4 — The Product
Three-step mobile workflow:
1. Start pre-inspection
2. Review findings
3. Receive inspection-readiness report

## Slide 5 — Market Validation & Size
Directional qualitative findings: 62% failed inspection/rework theme, 54% timeline uncertainty, 46% code interpretation. Modeled TAM ~$12B, SAM ~$255M, SOM ~$2.5M.

## Slide 6 — Competitive Landscape
Municipal systems, construction-management tools, AI inspection products, and private inspectors each solve parts of the workflow. InspectReady explores an integrated contractor-facing readiness experience.

## Slide 7 — Business Model
Hypotheses: SaaS subscription, pay per inspection, private-inspector commission, and premium drone add-on.

## Slide 8 — Next Steps
Interview contractors and inspectors, validate willingness to pay, choose an MVP inspection category, assess code-data access, and run a small pilot.
