# Market Sizing

The project used a modeled bottom-up TAM / SAM / SOM framework.

## TAM

**~$12B annual modeled opportunity**

This is a high-level long-term estimate for the broader contractor inspection/compliance workflow and depends on assumptions about customer counts and annual software value.

## SAM

**~$255M annual modeled opportunity**

Initial target: U.S. residential building contractors.

Model:

- ~212,000 contractor establishments used as a proxy
- × ~$1,200 assumed annual value per customer
- ≈ $255M

## SOM

**~$2.5M potential ARR**

Illustrative early target:

- ~2,100 customers
- × ~$1,200 assumed annual value
- ≈ $2.5M ARR

A more conservative milestone used in the project was:

- 500 customers
- × $1,200
- = ~$600K ARR

## Important limitation

These are modeled estimates for decision-making, not confirmed market revenue. The customer count, target segment, pricing, and obtainable share require primary research.
