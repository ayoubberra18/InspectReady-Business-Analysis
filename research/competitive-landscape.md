# Competitive Landscape

The project found that inspection readiness sits across several existing product categories rather than one single type of competitor.

| Category | Examples | What they solve | Remaining opportunity explored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Municipal systems | Accela, OpenGov | Permits, scheduling, municipal workflows | Contractor-side preparation and readiness |
| Construction management | Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud | Project management, documentation, collaboration | Dedicated pre-municipal inspection workflow |
| Digital inspection/checklists | SafetyCulture | Checklists, evidence capture, reports | Local-code-aware readiness assistance |
| Emerging AI inspection | Specta, Infrava Inspect, Tradei Vision | AI-assisted field/code review | Differentiation through workflow, trust, human escalation, drone evidence |
| Human/private inspectors | Various local providers | Professional inspection judgment | Fast, integrated digital access and escalation |

## Positioning hypothesis

A possible differentiation is to combine:

**Phone / drone capture → AI-assisted findings → human escalation when needed → inspection-readiness report**

This remains a hypothesis and should be tested against current competitor capabilities and contractor preferences.
