# Case Study — InspectReady

## Business question

Can a pre-inspection readiness workflow reduce the operational cost and uncertainty contractors experience before municipal building inspections?

## My role

As a Student Consultant, I worked across the full early-stage analysis cycle: problem framing, qualitative research, market sizing, competitor analysis, ideation, prototyping, and final recommendation.

## Analytical work

I converted an initially broad idea into a testable business problem, identified a primary user, documented assumptions, examined current alternatives, analyzed recurring customer complaints, and modeled the market opportunity using TAM / SAM / SOM.

## Product work

I used Crazy 8's to explore multiple solution mechanisms and then developed a three-step prototype centered on pre-inspection setup, issue identification, and an inspection-readiness report.

## Decision-making takeaway

The most important lesson was not simply how to generate a solution. It was how to take incomplete and messy information, identify what matters, distinguish evidence from assumptions, surface risks, and decide what should be tested next.

## Current recommendation

Do not build the full product yet. First validate:

- frequency and cost of failed inspections,
- willingness to pay,
- contractor trust in AI-assisted recommendations,
- which inspection type creates the strongest MVP entry point,
- and whether jurisdiction-specific code data can be used reliably.
